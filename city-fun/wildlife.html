Wildlife

Here in Heaven City, we pride ourselves on the respectful relationship we have with our wildlife. Full of incredible biodiversity, our wildlife consists of over 7000 different species' of animals, plants, and insects who are all considered protected under Heaven's eco-wildlife preservative initiative. This initiative protects all native species that live in and around our city, and ensures that Heaven's natural ecosystem remains safe from invasive species and disease by changing the city's laws on hunting yearly. These laws get updated to reflect the current state of Heaven's wildlife population, and require an annual renewal of Heaven's Hunting License. Hunting in Heaven is only permitted for specified invasive species that our wildlife specialists identify, and is actually encouraged by our city to restore our ecosystem to a green status.
