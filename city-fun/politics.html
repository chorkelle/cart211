Politics

In Heaven City we pride ourselves on our diverse political system that supports a multitude of platforms in order to ensure the happiness of our citizens. Our government is comprised of 67 members that all represent a set of unique policies. Our government is heavily supported by our very own Heaven Police Department - HPD - who ensure the safety and comfort of you. We owe our safety to these brave individuals lead by HPD Commissioner Fucke A.I.

Our government spends a lot of our resources ensuring the safety of our wildlife remains uncompromised in a world full of global warming.
